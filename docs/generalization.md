# Generalization experiments — companion repository

Does the single honesty steering direction (extracted in [vector_generation.md](vector_generation.md))
generalize out of distribution? Four held-out settings: **MASK**, **Among Us** (impostor win
rate — no ELO), **AuditBench** (SFT + KTO cohorts), and **Emergent Misalignment**.

**These four pipelines are released separately.** Their code and runbook entries live in the
**companion repository** — run and consult them there. Together they produce the generalization
results reported in Section 5.4 of the paper.
